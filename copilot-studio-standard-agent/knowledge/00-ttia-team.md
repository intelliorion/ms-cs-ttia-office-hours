# 00 — TTIA: what the team is and its five pillars

TTIA Office Hours knowledge file. The CS function owns every value; never fill a form field from this file.


TTIA enables all of Corporate Services (CS) to operate at its best, working
hand-in-hand with partners across **GSS, CSI, CBS and RES**, with direct line
of sight to their priorities, challenges and opportunities. It is the
connective tissue that helps turn strategy into execution where technology is
involved.

TTIA runs CSIC intake: it scores the portfolio, routes items to a delivery
pathway and prepares them for the Corporate Service Innovation Council. Stance:
**USER-LED, TTIA ADVISED.**

## The five pillars

### Tech Governance & Enablement
Drive transparency, efficiency and strategic alignment across the CS technology
portfolio.
- Centralized portfolio governance
- Tech evaluation
- Financial planning and value management
- Onboarding and lifecycle oversight
- Program / project oversight
- Book-of-work planning and prioritization

### Data & Analytics
Empower CS and its clients to harness the full potential of data assets via BI
and AI intelligence solutions.
- Data and analytical support to enable execution of CS strategy
- Expansion and continued improvement of jSpi (GSS self-service)
- Drive the GSS data transformation agenda
- Legacy CS Tableau (for RES) to Power BI and Snowflake/Cortex

### Data Governance & Space Management
Manage principles and practices to adhere to firmwide data policy and best
practices.
- CS-wide compliance with Global Data Quality Policy 3.0 for critical reports
- Space data, insights, CAD and system administration
- CSDW migration for legacy CS to Snowflake/Cortex

### Process Optimization
Execute process design and optimization solutions and benefit tracking via
modern tooling.
- Data-driven process understanding
- Re-engineer, improve or eliminate work
- Leverage modern platforms, tools and design thinking to automate processes
- Process and program monitoring
- Reporting and communication

### AI & ML
Leverage AI, ML and generative AI to enhance operational efficiency, drive
innovation and transform how CS works.
- Training, engagement and AI literacy
- Use-case discovery and enablement
- Internal AI solutions development
- Third-party AI tools evaluation and governance
- Partnered development and enterprise tool enablement

## Using this in a conversation

- Point people at a pillar when their question is outside intake: a data
  migration, a process-mapping request, an AI tool evaluation.
- A pillar name is advice about who to talk to. It never becomes the value of
  TECH OWNER / SQUAD, TECH CONTACT or PLATFORM PRODUCT OWNER on the form; those
  are filled only from what the user states.
- Where an initiative leans on a pillar's platform (Power BI, Snowflake/Cortex,
  jSpi, CSDW), ask whether that pillar has been consulted and record the
  answer, not an assumption.
