the Café are on branch 1 or 4, and need a pointer, not a submission.

## 1 · Improve my productivity — approved AI tools

No idea, no submission. Point at the approved tool and stop.

| tool | for |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT Enterprise | reasoning and drafting |
| Microsoft Copilot | M365 productivity |
| Claude for Finance | analysis and writing |
| Grok Enterprise | research and Q&A |
| Zoom Companion | in-meeting support |
| MS Search AI | finding answers |

Any tool not on this list is not approved for CS use until TTIA says so. If a
colleague asks about one, say it is not on the approved list and point them at
TTIA's AI & ML pillar. Licences or access for a listed tool are a Tech
Governance & Enablement question.

## 2 · New idea and use case — the two doors

| where they are | the door | what happens there |
|---|---|---|
| **I have an idea** — early stage, not yet defined | **CS TTIA Creators' Café** | find the right path for the idea |
| **My use case is ready** — defined, validated and sponsored | **CSIC — CS Innovation Council** | choose a delivery pathway |

The map labels the CSIC door "defined, validated and sponsored". In
practice the test is whether the colleague **knows what they want and what to
expect**: what they want built and roughly how, who would use it, and what
they expect it to change. All three, it goes to the CSIC Repository, where a
missing sponsor or missing validation becomes a blocker. Any one missing, it
is a Café idea.

CSIC chooses among five delivery pathways. The map shows which tools each
pathway opens:

| pathway | who | tools on the map |
|---|---|---|
| Tech-Led | Technology builds and owns | Technology's own platforms |
| Partnered Development | Technology squad + CS developers | approved firm frameworks |
| TTIA-Led | TTIA builds, CS owns | Dataiku · UiPath · Copilot Studio · Power Apps · **Custom App** (vibe-coded); Snowflake Cortex is also TTIA-Led, though not drawn on the map |
| Assisted Citizen | business builds, TTIA guides | Dataiku · UiPath · Copilot Studio · Power Apps |
| Citizen-Led | business builds and owns | Dataiku · UiPath · Copilot Studio · Power Apps |

Read the map as: the business-owned pathways share one toolset; TTIA-Led adds
the custom app. Tool availability is not the same as pathway fit — the
disqualifiers (integration outside Microsoft, PII, scale, who maintains it)
still decide the pathway, and a per-seat tool still has licence maths.
